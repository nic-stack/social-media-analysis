About the data & how to read this dashboard
  • Posts included: organic feed posts on Instagram, LinkedIn and Facebook from Jan 2025 to Oct 2026, at least 14 days old at export. Excluded: Stories, 47 ad-only (“dark”) posts, TikTok (5 posts), and Facebook posts reporting 0 impressions.
  • Interactions = likes + comments + shares on every platform (Instagram saves are not included). Interaction rate = interactions ÷ impressions, calculated per post, then the median.
  • Medians, not averages: results are heavily skewed. One LinkedIn post had about 300,000 impressions.
  • Platforms define metrics differently. Compare topics and formats within a platform. Cross-platform comparisons are most reliable on the same-post chart.
  • Topics and the student-focused flag come from keyword rules applied to captions. They are analysis additions; 88% are high-confidence. Spot-check before quoting exact counts.
  • Clicks (LinkedIn, Facebook) include clicks on images and “see more”, not only website visits. Instagram doesn’t report post clicks.
  • Differences show association, not cause: topic, format and timing overlap. Small groups (under 10 posts) are directional only.
  • Privacy: captions, links, IDs, exact dates and follower counts are not included in this page.
  • No ad spend, website traffic or application data is included.
